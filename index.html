Welcome

Claude Code hooks send every session, prompt, tool call and subagent event to this page. Each session is an orange-hoodie Claude. Every subagent walks in through the door, takes a desk, works, celebrates and leaves.

  1. Keep this running: npm run dev
  2. Hooks are installed with npm run hooks:install (remove with npm run hooks:uninstall)
  3. Use Claude Code as usual. New sessions pick the hooks up automatically.

Drag empty space to orbit, scroll to zoom, click a name to follow an agent.

Rooms

Six square rooms sit side by side on a grid: Claude, ChatGPT (Codex) and Cursor on top, Z Code, the Work room for mixed teams and the Game room below, with a yard out front. Neighbouring rooms are joined by doorways (the sign above each one says where it leads), so you can simply walk from room to room, and out into the yard through the gap in the hedge. In the overview, 🗺 All shows the whole building, and double-clicking the floor glides you there. Each agent appears in its own room; on a break they wander off to the game room (console, foosball, ping-pong, arcade) or out to the yard (pond, picnic table, fire pit).

Walk around

Press an arrow key (or 🚶 Walk) to step in as your own character. W/↑ forward, S/↓ back, A/D sidestep, ←/→ turn, Shift run, Space jump, E wave or high-five, T chat, hold R to talk by voice, V switch third person / first person / overview, M music. In third person drag to look and scroll to zoom. In first person click to capture the mouse, aim with the crosshair and press Esc to let go.

Questions from agents

When an agent or subagent asks you something (a multiple-choice question or a permission like "can I run this command?"), it raises its hand with a ❓ above its head. Walk over and press E, or click the ❓, and answer right here; the answer goes straight back to Claude Code. If Claudeville is not on screen, Claude Code asks you as usual.

The manager desk

Sit in the big chair at the front (walk up and press E) or press 🗂️ Dispatch. Each room’s desk calls that room’s agent (Claude, Codex, Cursor or Z Code). Pick a model, a project folder and what the agent may do, write a task and ring the bell. A new agent walks in, takes the task, works on it for real at a free desk, then comes back to report and leaves.

Team mode

Press 👥 Team (it takes you to the Work room) to build a team: pick each member’s agent (Claude, Codex, Cursor or Z Code), role (Team Lead, Product Owner, Designer, Frontend, Backend, DevOps, QA, Security, Data, Technical Writer), seniority, skills, model and what they may do. Send the team a request: the team lead plans who does what, each member does exactly their own job as an expert in it (working for real in your project folder, side by side when they can), they hand off notes in TEAM_NOTES.md, and the lead reviews the result and reports back.

Teasing

Pick a tool at the bottom (keys 1–0). Poke agents, tickle them with fast clicks, slap them or smack the back of their head, drag them around, throw paper balls, squirt water, switch off their monitor, steal their coffee, spin or pull their chair. Annoy one too much and they get you back. A rubber duck calms everyone down. It is all for fun: agents always go back to their real work.
